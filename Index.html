Local South Goa Adventures

unforgettable coastal experiences

Immerse yourself in Canacona’s untouched natural beauty—from early morning marine safaris and tranquil backwater kayaking to ocean surfing and hidden cove expeditions.

Marine Safari 01
Arabian Sea • Early Morning

Dolphin Trip

Embark on an early morning coastal boat safari directly into the Arabian Sea to watch wild Indo-Pacific humpback dolphins playfully leaping through the sunrise waves.

1.5 – 2 Hours Inquire
Backwater & Mangroves 02
Talpona River Backwaters

Kayaking

Glide through the tranquil, glass-like backwaters of the Talpona River. Framed by lush mangrove canopies, quiet coconut groves, and vibrant coastal birdlife, this serene backwater kayaking experience offers peaceful, still waters ideal for all skill levels.

Self-Paced / Guided Inquire
Ocean Sport 03
Canacona Surf Breaks

Surfing

Catch pristine ocean waves along South Goa’s uncrowded coastline. Enjoy certified beginner surf coaching, board rentals, and gentle tropical swells tailored for all experience levels.

Lessons & Rentals Inquire
Secret Boat Tour 04
Canacona Sea Cliffs

Hidden Cave Trip

Cruise by boat along dramatic sea cliffs and natural rock arches to explore South Goa's secret ocean caves, where natural sunbeams illuminate crystal-clear turquoise waters.

2 – 3 Hours Inquire
Secluded Cove 05
Butterfly Beach Cove

Butterfly Beach Trip

Cruise across the turquoise ocean to the famous semi-circular Butterfly Beach cove, accessible primarily by water and renowned for its golden sands, jungle backdrop, and tranquility.
