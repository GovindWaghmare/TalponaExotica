Resort Policies & Guidelines

clear guest policies

Essential information regarding check-in timings, child occupancy, payments, and property conditions to ensure a tranquil stay for all guests.

Last Updated: November 2026

1. General Booking & Reservation Conditions

Welcome to Talpona Exotica. By making a reservation enquiry or booking accommodation at our boutique resort, you agree to comply with the policies, terms, and guidelines set forth below. These terms ensure a peaceful, safe, and enjoyable stay for all guests.

Talpona Exotica maintains an intimate inventory of ten guest rooms. All reservations are subject to room availability and formal written acknowledgement from our front desk reception team.

2. Check-In & Check-Out Timings

  • Standard Check-in Time: 14:00 (2:00 PM).
  • Standard Check-out Time: 11:00 AM.
  • 24-Hour Reception: Our reception desk operates 24 hours daily to receive guests arriving on late flights or train journeys.
  • Early Arrival & Late Departure: Early check-in or late check-out requests are subject to room availability on the date of arrival/departure and may attract additional hourly charges.

3. Guest Identification Requirements

In compliance with local government regulations in Goa, India, all adult guests (Indian and international) must present valid original government-issued photo identification upon arrival:

  • Indian Nationals: Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driver’s License (PAN cards are not accepted as proof of residence).
  • International Guests: Valid Passport with valid Indian Visa or OCI card, and completion of standard Form C registration.

4. Child & Extra Bed Policies

  • Children Under 7 Years: Stay complimentary when sharing existing bedding with parents, subject to room category capacity.
  • Extra Beds: Extra folding beds or mattresses are available on request for applicable room categories at additional charges per night.
  • Maximum Room Capacities:
    • Panoramic Sea View Suite with Jacuzzi: 2 Adults + 1 Child
    • Partial Sea View Deluxe Room: 2 Adults + 1 Child
    • Twin Deluxe Room: 2 Adults
    • Deluxe Family Room: 4 Adults
    • Sea View Deluxe Room: 2 Adults + 1 Child

5. Advance Payment & Guarantee

To confirm and secure any room reservation, a minimum advance deposit of 50% of the total booking value is required. The balance 50% payment, along with any incidentals, is payable upon arrival during check-in.

6. Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Standard Cancellation: Free cancellation is permitted up to 30 days prior to the scheduled arrival date. Advance deposits will be refunded less any applicable banking or transaction handling fees.
  • Late Cancellation: Cancellations made within 30 days of the scheduled arrival date attract a 100% room-rate cancellation fee. No refunds will be issued for cancellations made within this period.
  • Peak Season Non-Refundable Bookings: All reservations falling between 15 December and 15 January (inclusive) are strictly non-refundable and non-amendable.
  • No-Shows & Early Check-Out: In the event of a no-show or early departure, the entire booked stay tariff remains chargeable without refund.

7. Applicable Taxes & GST

Goods and Services Tax (GST) is levied in accordance with current Indian tax statutes:

  • 5% GST on room tariffs below ₹7,500 per night.
  • 18% GST on room tariffs of ₹7,500 and above per night.

* Precise tax treatments are confirmed on your reservation voucher prior to payment.

8. Property Policies: Smoking, Pets & Amenities

  • Non-Smoking Policy: All indoor guest rooms at Talpona Exotica are strictly non-smoking. Smoking is permitted only in designated outdoor open-air areas.
  • Pet Policy: In order to maintain peaceful, allergy-free conditions for all guests, pets are strictly not permitted on property grounds.
  • Jacuzzi Usage: Private jacuzzis are exclusive to the two Panoramic Sea View Suites only.
  • Balcony Safety: All rooms except the Deluxe Family Room feature private balconies. Guests must exercise caution on balconies, especially with young children.
  • Parking: On-site secure parking for up to 12 vehicles is available complimentary, subject to space availability on arrival.
  • Power Backup: Continuous generator backup operates to maintain essential lighting and comforts during municipal outages.

9. Liability & Guest Conduct

Guests are expected to conduct themselves in a considerate manner, respecting the tranquil coastal atmosphere of Talpona. Talpona Exotica is not liable for loss or damage to personal valuables left unattended in rooms or common areas.
