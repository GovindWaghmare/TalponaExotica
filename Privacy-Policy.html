Guest Data Privacy & Protection

your trust matters

A transparent breakdown of how we collect, handle, and safeguard your personal details when booking or contacting our resort.

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Talpona Exotica ("we", "our", or "the Resort"), located at Talpona, Canacona, South Goa, India, values your trust and is committed to protecting the privacy of our website visitors and guests. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning the collection, use, and protection of information submitted through our website (https://talponaexotica.com/).

2. Information We Collect

When you browse our website, make an enquiry, or interact with our team, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact Information: Full name, mobile telephone number, and email address.
  • Stay Enquiry Details: Requested check-in and check-out dates, number of accompanying adults and children, preferred room category, and any special requests.
  • Technical Browsing Data: Standard browser user-agent, device screen resolution, and anonymous usage patterns to optimize performance across devices.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information provided to Talpona Exotica is used exclusively for legitimate hospitality operations:

  • To respond to your reservation enquiries and provide current tariff and room availability details.
  • To coordinate check-in procedures, dietary preferences, or airport and railway transfer assistance.
  • To communicate important stay updates directly related to your reservation.
  • To ensure website security and prevent spam or fraudulent submissions.

4. WhatsApp & Direct Messaging Communication

Our availability checker and booking enquiry forms facilitate direct communication with our front desk team via WhatsApp (+91 95119 39119). When you initiate a message via WhatsApp, your message and telephone number are subject to WhatsApp's standard end-to-end encryption and terms of service. We do not sell or distribute your phone number to third-party telemarketers.

5. Email Communication

If you send an enquiry to our official email address (stays@talponaexotica.com), your correspondence is stored securely and accessed solely by authorized reservations personnel for reservation management.

6. Third-Party Services & Links

Our website may contain external links to Google Maps for location directions or third-party web services. Talpona Exotica is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

This website does not deploy invasive tracking cookies or targeted marketing trackers. Standard temporary session storage may be utilized solely to preserve functional user interface preferences (such as mobile menu state or date pickers).

8. Data Security & Retention

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect guest data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Reservation correspondence is retained only for the period necessary to fulfil booking management, accounting compliance, and guest history reference.

9. Guest Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal contact details we hold regarding your bookings, request corrections, or ask for the deletion of your enquiry data once your stay is concluded, subject to statutory record-keeping regulations.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact our management team:
